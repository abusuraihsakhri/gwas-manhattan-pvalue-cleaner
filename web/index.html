Interpretation

Genome-wide significance is shown at p < 5 × 10⁻⁸ as a conventional reference, not an automatic study-specific threshold. λGC = median(χ²₁) / 0.4549364. Polygenicity, population structure, LD and sample size affect its interpretation.
